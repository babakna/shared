Run your own numbers

Stage 1's calculators are embedded here, grouped by question; the directory tabs list every other calculator in the course (173 in all) and link to the module where each one lives. Each one computes from its stated formula; the historical tools use 98 years of US market data (Damodaran, NYU Stern, 1928–2025). The last tabs link to Babak's full-featured planning tools and to official calculators.

RememberCalculators show what the assumptions imply. They do not know your whole situation, and history is not a forecast.

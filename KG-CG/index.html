Two graphs, two jobs. A knowledge graph holds what is always true about a system; a context graph holds what is true about one thing, right now. Learn how each is built, how they bind together, and how that pairing turns a pile of alarms into a defensible answer.

Built for engineers, architects, and analysts who keep hearing “knowledge graph” without ever being shown one. Start from zero — no graph database experience assumed.

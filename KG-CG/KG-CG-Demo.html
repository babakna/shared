What this shows

Knowledge graph — the durable model: agency account, Priority Responder plan and SLA, slice and sessions, cells, SMF and UPF, the Nchf routes and CHF pools, then mediation, the CDR store, rating and billing. Plus the change records that touched them. It persists across every incident.

Context graph — what the system knows about this incident: observations, evidence pointing back at knowledge-graph entities, hypotheses and proposed actions, each with a source and a confidence. Created when the anchor fires, discarded on Reset.

Driving the demo

Pick a scenario from the blue selector in the header, or step through them with ‹ and ›. Then Play to walk the stages, or Step to advance while you narrate. Speed sets the dwell time. Arrow keys step, space plays and pauses.

Sizing it for your display

Drag the vertical handle between the topology and the detail panels to rebalance them. Double-click the handle to reset it, or focus it and use the arrow keys. The detail panels reflow into two columns once you give them the room.

Zoom scales the diagram in real pixels, so the labels never shrink below a readable size — the pane scrolls instead. Fit width fills the pane and keeps tracking it as you drag the handle or resize the window. Fit all shows the whole diagram with no scrolling, which will make it smaller. + and − zoom. Expand hides the detail panels entirely.

Un-reached stages in the narrowing rail stay blank until you get to them. The right-hand panels appear as the case file earns them: the live overlay, then the ranked candidates, then the action gate.

Reading the graph

Blue is the current candidate set. Faded entities were ruled out by an earlier stage but stay on screen so the narrowing is visible. Violet is the located root cause. Amber dashed boxes along the bottom are change records, linked to the entities they targeted.

Scores

Candidate scores are evidence-match scores against SME-authored failure fingerprints, not probabilities. A candidate is action-eligible only above threshold and only after the guardrail checks pass. Three actions are permanently blocked from automation in every scenario: customer credit posting, direct billing-state writes, and RAN firmware rollback on cells serving an active public-safety response.

Renaming entities

Settings → Rename entities replaces any entity label with your own, everywhere it appears including the evidence prose. Stored in this browser only.

Scope

Illustrative and vendor-neutral. The operator is generic: PLMN 001-01 is the 3GPP test network, the market code and every element name are invented, and vendors are deliberately unnamed. All identifiers, counters, agency names, thresholds, timestamps and change records are fictional and represent no real network, product, customer or operational data. Charging-plane roles and interfaces follow 3GPP TS 32.240, 32.255, 32.290 and 32.291.
